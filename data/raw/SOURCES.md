# Source documents (public)

These files are copies of public regulatory documents, kept here so the container image can be built without
downloading them (publisher sites often block cloud build servers). They are unchanged; their SHA-256 hashes are
checked by `scripts/download_sources.py`, and the official URLs are listed in `app/ingest/sources.py`. The official
documents always prevail.
